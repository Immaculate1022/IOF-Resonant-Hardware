### 2. The Möbius Cassette: Secure Modular Element
The antenna element is contained within a modular cartridge known as the **Möbius Cassette**, designed with advanced security features for military and high-security applications.

*   **Anti-Reverse Engineering (ARE)**: Critical components within the cassette, such as the electret-coated Möbius strip and holographic patterns, are protected by several layers of anti-tamper technology:
    *   **Irreversible Polymerization**: The electret material is designed to degrade or neutralize its charge irreversibly upon exposure to air, light, or specific electromagnetic fields (e.g., from scanning attempts).
    *   **Micro-Encapsulated Corrosive Agent**: A sealed micro-capsule is integrated to release a corrosive agent upon unauthorized physical intrusion or removal, destroying the electret coating and the Möbius strip.
    *   **Physical Obfuscation**: Internal components are fully encapsulated in an opaque, hard-to-remove epoxy resin, making non-destructive analysis impossible. Any attempt at physical access results in the destruction of critical elements.
*   **Field-Swappable (Securely)**: While designed for single-use per deployment, the modular nature allows for quick and secure replacement with a new, authorized cassette, optimized for different operational parameters (e.g., varying electret charge densities or holographic coating patterns).

## 🔬 Operating Principles
### Claim 1: The Rotating Möbius Element
The primary antenna element is a Möbius strip—a non-orientable surface with a single boundary—coated with an **electret material**.
*   **The Electret**: A dielectric material that holds a permanent electric charge (the electrostatic equivalent of a permanent magnet).
*   **Mechanical Current**: By rotating this static charge at a constant angular velocity, a time-varying magnetic field is generated. This creates the required ELF/ULF transmission components without the need for massive electrical currents.

### Claim 2: Topological Advantage
The Möbius topology ensures a continuous and non-linear charge distribution. As the strip rotates, the field generated is hypothesized to be significantly more uniform and efficient than that produced by a simple rotating disk or loop, maximizing the magnetic dipole moment within a small volume.

### Claim 3: Precision Control
A high-precision control system (utilizing a Phase-Locked Loop or PLL) maintains the rotational stability required for a clean, narrow-band signal. For example, a 7 Hz ULF signal requires exactly 7 rotations per second.
