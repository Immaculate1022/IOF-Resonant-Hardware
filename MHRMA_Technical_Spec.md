## 🔬 Operating Principles
### Claim 1: The Rotating Möbius Element
The primary antenna element is a Möbius strip—a non-orientable surface with a single boundary—coated with an **electret material**.
*   **The Electret**: A dielectric material that holds a permanent electric charge (the electrostatic equivalent of a permanent magnet).
*   **Mechanical Current**: By rotating this static charge at a constant angular velocity, a time-varying magnetic field is generated. This creates the required ELF/ULF transmission components without the need for massive electrical currents.

### Claim 2: Topological Advantage
The Möbius topology ensures a continuous and non-linear charge distribution. As the strip rotates, the field generated is hypothesized to be significantly more uniform and efficient than that produced by a simple rotating disk or loop, maximizing the magnetic dipole moment within a small volume.

### Claim 3: Precision Control
A high-precision control system (utilizing a Phase-Locked Loop or PLL) maintains the rotational stability required for a clean, narrow-band signal. For example, a 7 Hz ULF signal requires exactly 7 rotations per second.
